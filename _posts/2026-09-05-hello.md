This site has been a resume with two extra pages. That is enough for a hiring loop, and not enough if I want a record of what I actually learned while building something.

I am going to use this log for short notes: systems work, project decisions, and the occasional dead end. No cadence. If a post is here, I had something concrete to write down.

Recent context, if you are new: CS at Colorado School of Mines, a summer at Checkr on invoicing workflows, and a handful of smaller projects (ClubHub, a terminal Tetris clone, the old Lizard Repairs booking site). The [projects page](/projects.html) is the index; this is the working notes.
