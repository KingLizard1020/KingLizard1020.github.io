Product

ClubHub · Python, Streamlit, SQLite

A club operations app with two Streamlit surfaces: an admin dashboard and a member portal. SQLite stores prospects, members, events, RSVPs, and attendance as separate tables, so interest can be collected before someone becomes a member. Admins convert prospects, manage events, and track check-ins; members RSVP and check in themselves.
