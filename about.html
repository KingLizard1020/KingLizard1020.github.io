About

I am a software engineer in Golden, Colorado. I studied Computer Science with a minor in Electrical Engineering at Colorado School of Mines, and I care about systems that stay correct when the happy path is not the only path.

In summer 2026 I interned at Checkr, where I moved monthly customer invoicing onto Temporal and replaced a production-console close process with a self-service operator UI. Before that I spent six years running Lizard Repairs, an electronics repair shop, including the booking site customers used to schedule work. After I closed the shop I moved that site to Wix so it would stay online without keeping the custom domain.

I have also studied abroad at Budapest University of Technology and SKEMA Business School. If you want the one-page version, the home page is the resume, and you can download the PDF.
